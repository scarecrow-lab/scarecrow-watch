Privacy & data

Scarecrow Watch is built to need as little of your data as possible — ideally none.

← Dashboard Privacy About

In short: no accounts, no server, no cookies, and no third-party trackers or analytics. We don't collect personal data about you. The only things stored are your language and theme choices — kept in your own browser, never sent to us.

What we store

  • Your preferences, in your browser only. Your chosen language and light/dark theme, and whether you've seen the intro screen, are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are cleared when you clear your browser's site data.
  • No cookies, no tracking. We set no cookies and run no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts. We don't profile visitors or build audiences.
  • No sign-in. There are no accounts and no login, so there's nothing to collect about who you are.

The feedback button

The site has no server to receive form submissions. The Feedback & grievances button simply opens your own email app with a pre-filled draft — we only ever receive what you choose to write and send. Email is not anonymous: please don't include names, exact locations, or details that could put you or others at risk. For anything sensitive, ask us for a secure channel first.

Hosting & logs

The site is a set of static files hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, the hosting provider may automatically log standard technical request information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operations. We don't control, use, or combine those logs, and we add no tracking of our own on top. External resources we load (map tiles, fonts, flag icons) are served by their providers under their own terms.

The data we show

Risk assessments combine the Lab's judgement with public data aggregated from sources such as UCDP, UNHCR, the World Bank, V-Dem, ReliefWeb and GDELT, each attributed. We document patterns and verified public information — not graphic imagery of victims, and not personal data about private individuals. Protected material (tips, witnesses, case files) is never published here; map hotspots are shown at province level only.

Open by default

The code and the published data are open source on GitHub, and the data is downloadable from the dashboard. Anyone can inspect how the site works and what it stores.

Contact & changes

Questions about privacy or data can be sent through the feedback channel on the dashboard. If we make a material change to how the site handles data, we'll note it here.
