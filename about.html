About Scarecrow

Someone has to stand watch over the field.

Mass atrocities give warning, often months or years ahead. Scarecrow brings those warnings together for all 11 ASEAN states, openly and free of charge, so that communities and responders can act earlier.

Work with us How we are organised
01 · Structure

Two arms, one firewall

Scarecrow is designed as two separate bodies. The public-interest work never depends on, or answers to, anyone who pays for analysis.

The Lab owns all evidence. Only findings that are verified and already published can cross the firewall. Clients cannot see, commission, delay or stop Lab work.

02 · Safeguards

Rules we hold ourselves to

These are built into how the system works, not only written in a policy.

Sources stay protected

Tips, witness accounts and case files never appear publicly or reach any client.

Two people sign off

One analyst edits an assessment; a different analyst must approve it before it is published.

Big jumps wait for a human

When automated data moves a score sharply, the change is held until an analyst checks it.

No exact locations

Map hotspots are shown at province level, so no dot can point to a camp, village or safe house.

Clients can't steer the Lab

Anyone under active Lab investigation cannot become a client, and no client can commission or stop Lab work.

Funding disclosed yearly

We will publish our funders and the sectors of any clients every year.

03 · Method

From public data to a published assessment

Machines gather and flag; people judge. Every score links to the evidence behind it.

Frameworks: UN Framework of Analysis for Atrocity Crimes (8 risk factors) · AIPG PREVENT (7 risk categories)
