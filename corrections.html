How we handle errors

  • We correct promptly and visibly. If a published finding is wrong, we fix it, note what changed and when, and inform anyone materially affected.
  • Serious errors are retracted, with an explanation — never quietly deleted.
  • Every change is on the record. The platform already tracks score history month-on-month; substantive corrections are logged here so the history of an assessment is auditable.
  • Anyone can challenge a finding by pointing to evidence. We review good-faith challenges against the same standard we use to publish, and respond on the merits — regardless of who raises them.
  • A correction is a sign the system is working, not a failure to hide.

This log covers substantive corrections and retractions to published findings. Routine daily data refreshes from public sources are not listed here; month-on-month movements in each country's risk score are shown in the live dashboard under “What changed”. To report a possible error, use the feedback & grievances channel on the dashboard.
