Find the Missing

If you are searching for a missing or disappeared relative, or hold information about a crime, these organizations can help — safely and free. Scarecrow Watch does not collect this information; it points you to those who do.

← Dashboard
Your safety comes first. Use a safe device and connection before you share any name or detail. These services are confidential — a public post is not. If you may be at risk, contact the organization directly and never post names publicly.
ICRC — Restoring Family Links For families
Trace and reconnect with family separated by conflict or displacement, and seek news of the missing. Confidential, neutral and free, through the Red Cross Family Links network.
Open ↗
ICMP — Online Inquiry Center For families
Report a missing person and help identify the missing, including through DNA. The Online Inquiry Center lets families submit and seek information.
Open ↗
IIMM — UN Myanmar Mechanism For witnesses & NGOs
Share evidence or information about serious crimes in Myanmar through a secure, confidential channel. The Mechanism preserves it for future justice.
Open ↗
What Scarecrow Watch does — and does not do
We are an early-warning watch, not a tracing service or a registry of victims. We hold no personal data about the missing or their families. This page only links you to the specialised bodies that can help.

Part of Scarecrow Watch — an independent, non-profit, open-source early-warning watch for Southeast Asia. This page stores nothing and collects no personal data.
