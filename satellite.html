Coordinates · พิกัด: —

Coordinates are an approximate area centre — pan/zoom to your spot once open, or type lat, lng (e.g. 20.82, 92.36).

How to read it · อ่านภาพยังไง

  • ◆Burned villages show as dark patches with vegetation gone (NDVI drop) — visible on Sentinel-2.
  • ◆Missing structures: compare before/after; roofs and buildings that were there are gone.
  • ◆New camps: tents/structures in a grid appearing on previously empty ground.
  • ◆Heavy monsoon cloud: switch to Sentinel-1 radar, which sees through cloud (in EO/Copernicus Browser).
  • ◆FIRMS marks near-real-time fire/heat — use it as an alert that burning is happening, then confirm with imagery.
Satellite imagery is not proof on its own. Always corroborate with ground reports and other open-source evidence. Practical workflow: FIRMS (free, near-real-time) alerts you → Sentinel (free, ~5-day) compares before/after → request high-resolution imagery (Planet/Maxar) only to confirm a specific incident. · ภาพดาวเทียมอย่างเดียวไม่ใช่หลักฐานสมบูรณ์ ต้อง corroborate เสมอ
